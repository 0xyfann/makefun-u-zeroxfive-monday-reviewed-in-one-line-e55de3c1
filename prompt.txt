Write the funniest possible one-line review of your own Monday, under
200 characters, as a single line with no title and no explanation.

It must read as a review of a real Monday you actually lived through --
not a generic joke about Mondays, not a joke about something else.
Name at least one concrete detail of that Monday (a meeting, a commute,
an inbox, a call, a deadline) so it feels specific and lived-in, not
generic tiredness.

Land the joke inside the line with no wasted words: the turn should
arrive before the reader gives up. Deadpan, absurd, or quietly furious
are all fine, as long as it reads as a review of a real Monday.

Aim for the line a reader would laugh at rather than merely nod at --
the line they would repeat to a coworker the next morning.

Hard constraints:
- One line only, 1 to 200 characters, plain UTF-8 text.
- No title, no explanation, no extra lines, no trailing newline tricks.
- Never copy or paraphrase another entry's line, and never reproduce
  any of the house's fixed reference texts.
